Since 2014

WE DIDN'T WAIT FOR PERMISSION

Street Cult started in a one-bedroom apartment in East London. Two friends, a sewing machine and a vision: to make clothes that meant something — pieces that carried the energy of the streets they came from.

We weren't interested in fast fashion or trend-chasing. We wanted weight. Both literally — our fabrics average 380–420gsm — and culturally. Every collection is shaped by the music, art, and movement happening outside our studio windows.

Twelve seasons later, we're still making everything with that same deliberate intent. The city is our studio. The streets are our runway.

What We Stand For

OUR VALUES

Quality First

We use premium weight fabrics, tight stitching and rigorous quality control. If we wouldn't wear it ourselves, it doesn't leave the studio.

Cultural Authenticity

We draw from real street culture — not a boardroom's idea of it. Our design team lives and breathes the community we create for.

Ethical Production

All production partners are certified and audited annually. We pay fair wages and maintain full supply chain transparency.

Limited by Design

We drop in limited runs. No restocks, no mass production. When something's gone, it's gone — keeping every piece rare and meaningful.
